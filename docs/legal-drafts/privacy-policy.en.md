## What the site itself does

The site consists of static information pages. It has no accounts, sign-in, purchases, forms, comments or file uploads. The proposed purposes are delivering requested pages/resources and protecting the service's availability and integrity. Technical data is received and used while handling a request; information **may remain in technical logs after the response**. ## Your rights and requests

Under applicable law, you may obtain information about processing and access your data, request correction, blocking or destruction on statutory grounds, request cessation of processing, withdraw consent where it was the basis, and challenge the operator's actions before Roskomnadzor or a court. Continued retention on another lawful basis is assessed separately; a right to deletion does not mean unconditional deletion of every record.

Write to **mythweld@mythweld.com** describing your request and information linking it to your correspondence or data. Do not send a passport scan in an ordinary email. If a formal request or proof of authority is needed, the operator will explain the legal requirements and an appropriate way to provide minimal information. A formal access request requires the particulars and signature specified in Article 14(3) of Law No. 152-FZ; an ordinary email does not always replace it.

Information in response to an access request is provided within 10 working days; an extension of no more than 5 working days is allowed with a reasoned notice under Articles 14/20. Correction, cessation and deletion have the applicable time limits and grounds under Articles 20/21. This is not a response SLA for every product question.
